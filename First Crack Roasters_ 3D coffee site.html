Green to glossy in four steps.

  1. 1

    Cup at the farm

    We taste every lot where it's grown and only buy what we'd happily serve.

  2. 2

    Roast by ear

    Five-kilo batches in a drum roaster. We listen for first crack and drop the beans within seconds of target.

  3. 3

    Rest for 48 hours

    Fresh beans release CO₂. Resting lets the flavour settle so shots pull evenly.

  4. 4

    Brew to the gram

    Every shot is weighed in and out. If it runs outside 27 to 32 seconds, we pour it away and start again.
